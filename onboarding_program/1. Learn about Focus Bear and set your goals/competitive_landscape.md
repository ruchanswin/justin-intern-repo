# What makes Focus Bear different from these apps? (Freedom, Cold Turkey, Tiimo, Routinery)
* Focus Bear
- Primary Focus: Productivity + wellness app for neurodivergent users
- Neurodivergent Support: Designed for ADHD/Autism
- Routine Scaffolding: Includes exercise, meditation, etc.
- Distraction Blocking: Yes, with focus validation (e.g. YouTube educational vs. distracting content)
- Mood/Health Tracking: Includes wellness support (no detailed tracking)
- Platform Integration: Cross-platform (Windows, macOS, iOS, Android)
- UI/UX Design: Clean, functional interface
- Unique Features: Routine + wellness integration, ADHD-friendly pacing
- Pricing: $4.99/month

* Freedom
- Primary Focus: Distraction blocking
- Neurodivergent Support: No specific focus
- Routine Scaffolding: None
- Distraction Blocking: Strong cross-device app/website blocking
- Mood/Health Tracking: None
- Platform Integration: Cross-platform (Windows, Mac, iOS, Android, extensions)
- UI/UX Design: Highly polished
- Unique Features: Scheduled blocks across all devices
- Pricing: 7-day free trial, $39.99/year

* Cold Turkey
- Primary Focus: Strict blocking/digital detox
- Neurodivergent Support: No specific focus
- Routine Scaffolding: None
- Distraction Blocking: Yes – can block entire PC (Frozen Turkey mode)
- Mood/Health Tracking: None
- Platform Integration: Desktop only (Windows, macOS)
- UI/UX Design: Very basic
- Unique Features: Full lock mode – cannot override during blocks
- Pricing: One-time $59 for Pro

* Tiimo
- Primary Focus: Visual scheduling & reminders
- Neurodivergent Support: Designed for ADHD/Autism
- Routine Scaffolding: Routines with visual cues
- Distraction Blocking: Doesn’t block apps or sites
- Mood/Health Tracking: Syncs mood tracking with Apple Health
- Platform Integration: Cross-platform (iOS, Android, Apple Watch)
- UI/UX Design: Highly polished and user-friendly
- Unique Features: Widgets, calendar sync, health integration
- Pricing: 7-day free trial, $66/year or $13/month

* Routinery
- Primary Focus: Step-based habit routines
- Neurodivergent Support: Designed for everyone, ADHD-friendly
- Routine Scaffolding: Customizable routine timer
- Distraction Blocking: No blocking features
- Mood/Health Tracking: None
- Platform Integration: iOS and Android only
- UI/UX Design: Simple but can be unresponsive (e.g., issues with iPhone Dynamic Island)
- Unique Features: Multi-language support, widgets, add friends
- Pricing: 7-day free trial, $30/year
# If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over competitors?
- The reasons why I would prefer Focus Bear are: It does the habits with me, not just reminds me. It blocks distractions across all devices, so I don’t cheat by switching screens. It’s built by a team with ADHD, so the features feel empathetic and practical. It nudges me to take breaks at the right time, not randomly. It’s more than a blocker—it’s a routine enforcer, productivity coach, and wellness tracker rolled into one.
# What’s one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn’t?
- Cold Turkey’s “Frozen Turkey” mode locks you out of your entire computer for a set time—forcing full digital detox. Focus Bear doesn’t currently offer full system lockout (though it does lock screens during routines).
# Based on your research, what’s one improvement you think Focus Bear could make?
- More flexible scheduling and calendar integration. Apps like Tiimo and Routinery offer visual timelines, drag-and-drop scheduling, and routine previews. Focus Bear could benefit from a monthly calendar view, routine previews with adjustable time blocks, energy-based suggestions (like Tiimo’s AI co-planner). This would make it easier to plan ahead and adapt routines to changing energy levels or work demands.
