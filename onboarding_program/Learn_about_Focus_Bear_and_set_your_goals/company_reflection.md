# Why do you think Focus Bear was created?

- Focus Bear was created to enhance the productivity and concentration levels of its users, especially people who are diagnosed with ADHD or autism, by using an app to help build routines, manage distractions, and improve focus. The founder of Focus Bear, Jeremy, is also a diagnosed AuDHD himself, so he understands how ADHD and autistic people struggle with the everyday lives due to their difficulties in concentration. As shown by the statistics in the induction session, neurodivergent people are 40% less productive, 25% more likely to drop out of uni, and nearly 5 times more likely to commit suicides.

## What problem is Focus Bear solving?

- Focus Bear's main mission is to overcome the big barriers of neurodivergent individuals, which are their abilities to stay focus and be productive. The statistics show that roughly one-sixth of university students in Australia meet ADHD clinical thresholds, and it can worsen since students at uni have more deadlines to work on, more distractions appear and some students live far from their families, and their mental health can deteriorate. One main feature of the app is too block distractions from websites and apps (social media, games, etc.) when you want to focus on your work. The app uses AI-powered predictions to see if your viewing content aligns with your current goal (for example, you want to study for a math exam, so the app will check if your content is related to math). Besides, the app helps people build new routines and habits like working exercises, drinking water or taking short breaks after around 30 minutes of work to help you relax and get more energy for continuous work. These habits are proved to improve the concentration levels of neurodivergent people, which means Focus Bear is on the right track.

### Why do you think this mission is important?

- Because people who are diagnosed with ADHD or autism have increased, and the age range of diagnosed has broaden, which means there will be more people who are struggled with daily lives. Focus Bear can help people overcome the difficulties and adapt to ordinary academic or working lives, which can result in better productivity and increased mental health. The mission has a great impact on huge population, and it also helps spread awareness for everyone to provide enough support for neurodivergent people.
