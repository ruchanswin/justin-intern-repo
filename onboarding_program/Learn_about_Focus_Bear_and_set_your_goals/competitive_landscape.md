# What makes Focus Bear different from these apps? (Freedom, Cold Turkey, Tiimo, Routinery)
- Focus Bear is specially designed for neurodivergent people to improve concentration levels and productivity, which can improve their mental health.
- Rather than simple to-do lists, Focus Bear walks you real‑time through morning/evening routines—screen locked until you complete each step, with integrated video guidance and motivation.
- AI-powered distraction blocking via allow‑list: Besides manually blocking sites, Focus Bear learns your current goals and automatically blocks irrelevant apps/websites using AI-powered predictions.
- Focus Bear can be used across all devices and operating systems (Mac, Windows, iOS, Android), and support 2 languages (English and Spanish).
- Users of Focus Bear can postpone breaks appropriately; offers “Late No More” escalating alerts to prevent tardiness suited to ADHD needs.
- Focus Bear has built-in features to record habit completion, mood, energy levels, streaks, and leaderboard to encourage users to steadily build habits.
- Focus Bear's pricing plan is competitive: around $4.99/month with 7-day free trial and 85% discount for students.
- In conclusion, Focus Bear can integrate both routine tracing and distraction blocking, which not many products can provide.
In contrast:
* Freedom and Cold Turkey
- Freedom and Cold Turkey focus mainly on distraction blocking with manual lists and sessions, and they don't include habit building and routine tracking, along with AI-powered blocking. 
- While Freedom can be integrated across all devices like Focus Bear, Cold Turkey is only available via desktops. 
- The price of Freedom is $3.33/month for yearly subscription, $8.99/month for monthly subscription, and $99.5 for forever use with 7-day free trial. Freedom also has team plan.
- Cold Turkey's price is $59 for forever use with 7-day free trial, along with 20% discount for students.
* Tiimo and Routinery
- Tiimo and Routinery are habit/routine trackers without robust distraction‑blocking or AI‑assisted focus tools. Tiimo is also friendly for neurodivergent people like Focus Bear.
- Tiimo is available across all devices, with unique features like widgets and live activities. Tiimo has 2 pricing plan: $66/year for yearly subscription and $13/month for monthly subscription, along with 7-day free trial.
- Routinery is available on web and on mobile devices (no desktop apps). Routine's price is $US30/year with 7-day free trial, and supports 2 languages (English and Korean). It has a unique feature, which is making friends.
# If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over competitors?
- The reasons why I would prefer Focus Bear are: It combines both distraction blocking and routine tracking features, so I don't need to use multiple apps for multiple purposes. For neurodivergent users, since the founder of Focus Bear, Jeremy Nagel, is a neurodivergent individual himself, he can understand what users normally suffer and provide solutions for their everyday struggles. For students, the price is pretty good since with 85% student discount, the price for the whole year will only be around %18/year (To be fair, for longer use Cold Turkey and Freedom's forever plan is cheaper, but these plans have less flexibility and you have to use these apps for a considerable amount of time to make up for the cost: around 2.5 years for Cold Turkey with 20% student discount and around 5.5 years for Freedom).
# What’s one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn’t?
- Cold Turkey’s “Frozen Turkey” mode locks you out of your entire computer for a set time to enforce full digital detox.
- Routinery's feature to make friends (Focus Bear has Discord channels for communication so this feature is optional).
- Tiimo's feature for adding widgets and live activities.
# Based on your research, what’s one improvement you think Focus Bear could make?
- I think to compete with other apps, pricing is an important factor for customers to determine if they will spend their money to use the premium version of the apps. Currently, Focus Bear only has a monthly plan, so adding a yearly plan and a one-time payment plan is great. My suggested price for the yearly and forever plan is around $40-$45 for yearly plan and around $90-$95 for one-time payment plan (the price is cheaper than Freedom and a bit more expensive than Cold Turkey but Focus Bear is 2-in-1 app so the price is actually reasonable).
