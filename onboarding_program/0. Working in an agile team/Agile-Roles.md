# What are the key roles in an Agile team, and what does each role do?
- Developers are responsible for building the software systems, adding new features, testing the system output and maintaining the systems. They work with other roles for technical decisions, break down tasks into smaller, more manageable ones and deliver code frequently.
- QA (Quality Assurance) are responsible for testing the systems to monitor their performance and identify any bugs or errors with automated testing. They work with developers to ensure the output satisfies the requirements and all found bugs and errors have been fixed.
- Product Manager (PM) defines the product outlook and priorities based on user and business requirements, and manages the backlog to strengthen the bond between stakeholders and the team.
- Agile Project Manager (Agile PM) are responsible for holding Agile ceremonies, help the team set clear goals, update the progress of each member, reflect on achievements and room for improvements. 
- Other supporting roles (e.g., stakeholders, UX, customer support): UX designers conduct researches on what the users need and design the interface to satisfy users' requirements, stakeholders provide input requirements and give feedback on the output of the system, and customer support provide help when customers encounter any problems.

# How do these roles collaborate in Kanban vs. Scrum?
- Scrum: The team collaborate with each other based on Agile ceremonies. In daily stand-ups, they will share their progress, raise any questions and provide solutions. In sprint planning, they will set clear goals and objectives and these won't change until the next sprint. In retrospectives, they reflect on what have done well and what can be improved, which can boost confidence, trust and connections.
- Kanban: The collaboration is more flexible and every member can work in their own schedules and paces. The members can update their progress via message boards, and since the team doesn't have fixed plans effective communication among members is required to fulfil the requirements.
