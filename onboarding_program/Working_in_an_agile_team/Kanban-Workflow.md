# How does a Kanban board work, and how does it help manage workflow?
- The Kanban board is a visual board that helps the team keep track of the completion stages of tasks. It helps the team manage workflow since they can know which stages these tasks are in, if there are too many unfinished tasks or if not much progress has been made to start the tasks, or if they need to repriortise the tasks. This avoid situations where the progress is slow or the quality of work doesn't meet expectations.

# What do the different columns on a Kanban board represent? (e.g., Backlog, In Progress, Blocked, Done)
- Backlog: Tasks that are not started or waiting to be repriortised.
- Ready: Tasks that are ready to work on.
- In progress: Tasks that are continued working on.
- Blocked: Tasks that are postponed due to some issues.
- In review: Tasks that need to be reviewed to make sure they satisfy requirements.
- Done: Tasks that are completed after finishing reviewing and will be archieved for future reflection. 

# How do tasks move through the board, and who is responsible for updating them?
- Team members update the stages of their own tasks, the tasks move through the board from left to right.
- Agile PM or board owner can change the statuses of the board.

# What are the benefits of limiting work in progress (WIP)?
- To make sure there are no bottlenecks (too many tasks in progress).
- Improve focus, ensure steady workflow, completion rate and quality of work.
- Require team members to finish their tasks before moving on to the next ones.
- Avoid confusion and procastination due to a huge number of unfinished tasks.

# How does Kanban help manage priorities and avoid overload?
- The Kanban can visualize the progress of every task, so team members can see which tasks are unfinished (in progress) to divert priorities to them and avoid starting new tasks, and clear prioritises reduce overload since you know which tasks to be focused on and not being confused. Also, with Kanban board, you're free to work on the tassk at your own paces and schedules.

# How can you improve your workflow using Kanban principles?
- The Kaban principles can help me track the task progress, make sure I don't start too many tasks or leave many tasks unfinished by setting WIP to around 3-5 tasks. Also, if I get stuck with any tasks, I can set the status to Blocked to allow other members to offer help, or set the tasks aside to work on other ones and return to stuck tasks later.
