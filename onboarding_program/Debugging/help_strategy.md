# Spend 30 minutes talking with Chat GPT to understand different perspectives on using AI in coding. When is it helpful and when is it not?

- AI is helpful if you want to generate code and syntax quickly, explain different concepts, adding comments to your code, optimizing your code performance and aumotate repetitive tasks. It can provide instant feedback, help debug errors, and offer suggestions for best practices.
- AI may not be helpful for working on specific projects (academic, niche fields or projects containing confidential and sensitive data), can sometimes give incorrect or outdated answers due to halluncination, cannot replace human's crticial thinking or team collaboration for complex, and may be vulnerable to cyberattacks to exploit the AI tools' weaknesses.

# When do you prefer using AI vs. searching Google?

- I will use AI when I need a quick explanation, code generation, or help with a coding problem. AI is also useful for brainstorming and learning new concepts interactively.
- I will use Google when I need official documentation and credible sources for complex projects or writing academic reports, community discussions (Stack Overflow or Reddit), or to research broader issues and compare multiple solutions.

# How do you decide when to ask a colleague instead?

- I will ask a colleague when the problem is specific to our codebase and related to our roles, requires domain knowledge, or when I've tried looking for answers from AI chatbots or Google but still can't find a solution.
- I also seek help from my colleagues when I need them for code reviews, design decisions, and learning standard coding and communication conventions.

# What challenges do developers face when troubleshooting alone?

- Developers may get stuck in tunnel vision, overlook simple mistakes and struggle with complex problems, or waste too much time and effort on issues that could be solved faster with help.
- It can be hard to stay motivated, and it's easy to miss best practices or misunderstand requirements without enough support. Collaborating with other people can speed up problem-solving, lead to better solutions, keep you motivated and make sure your work meets coding standards.
