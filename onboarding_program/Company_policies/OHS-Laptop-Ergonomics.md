🔍 Research & Learn

# What are the risks of using a laptop without an external monitor or keyboard?
- It can lead to poor posture like hunched back and shoulders, neck strain from looking down on the laptops due to wrong placement of the laptops (if the laptops are placed at a low surface than eye levels), and eye strain from looking at the screen at a long time. It could lead to serious pains (neck, shoulder, back pains and headaches) and eye problems (dry eyes, blurred vision).

# What ergonomic equipment can improve posture when working on a laptop? (e.g., laptop stands, external keyboards, ergonomic chairs)
- Laptop stands: raises laptops to eye levels, which can improve postures.
- Ergonomic desks and chairs: supports back spine structure and reduces back pains.
- External keyboards and mouses: separate screen and typing fuctions.

# What adjustments should be made to monitor height, chair position, and desk setup for a healthier workspace?
- The top of the screen should be at eye levels or slightly lower. 
- Chairs should be placed so that the feet are on the floor and the knees create a right angle.
- Desks should be set up so that the elbows create a right angle when typing and the wrists are relaxed.

# What are some daily habits that reduce the impact of prolonged laptop use? (e.g., movement breaks, posture checks, eye strain reduction techniques)
- Frequent body stretches after each 30 minutes of working.
- Postures should make the body parts relaxed.
- Make some minor changes to your postures for blood circulation.
- 20-20-20 rule: For 20 minutes, look 20 feets away in 20 seconds.
