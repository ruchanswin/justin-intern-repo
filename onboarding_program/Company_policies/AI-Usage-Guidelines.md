🔍 Research & Learn

# What AI tools are typically used for your role?
- ChatGPT or Cursor for code suggestion and feedback.
- Copilot for brainstorming ideas and creating drafts.
- Grammarly for checking vocabulary and grammars.

# What are the benefits and risks of using AI in a professional setting?
- Benefits: Save time and efforts, help with brainstorming and offer new way of thinking or new solutions.
- Risks: Can generate inaccurate and biased information due to halluncination and biased training data, risks of data leakage, being over-reliant on AI tools.

# What types of information should never be entered into AI tools?
- Sensitive data like customers' personal information or business data, internal code, files or documentation.

# How can you fact-check and validate AI-generated content to ensure accuracy?
- Cross-check from trusted sources and your own knowledge, especially for academic and scientific information.
- Ask the AI bots to provide sources for their generated answers. Sometimes they can create non-existent sources.
- Run and test the code before merging it with the codebase.
- Ask your colleagues, supervisors or Jeremy to proofread the code or documentation.

📝 Reflection

# When should you use AI for assistance, and when should you rely on your own skills?
- AI is useful for generating drafts, brainstorming idea, doing repetitive tasks and suggesting code.
- You should work on your own when the tasks require critical thinking or creativity, and within specific knowledge domains. You should always fact-check AI-generated content to make sure it is suitable for the contexts and intents, and it works properly. It's best practice to ask AI tools to provide sources for their content so that you can use these sources to evaluate the answers and make sure the links and references are valid. You are the final say, so only consider AI-generated content as references, not the sole reason.

# How can you avoid over-reliance on AI while still benefiting from it?
- You should always double-check the AI-generated content before using it for academic or professional work. Ask them the references for their content, and review the sources to ensure they're credible and valid.
- Try to figure out everything you should do, then use AI tools to compare their work with yours. You can combine your ideas with theirs to finalize your content.
- Limit the use of AI tools for tasks containing human feelings or sensitive data since the empathy from AI tools is based on training data, not actual experience.
- Consider AI tools as your collaborators, not the final decision-makers.

# What steps will you take to ensure data privacy when using AI tools?
- You should never feed AI tools sensitive data like clients' private info, companies' databases and codebases.
- When you want to ask AI, use generic contexts instead of specific examples from your own codebases.
- If you want to use AI tools, you can set the tools locally so that the data only stays within your devices and not be sent to hosting organizations.
